The visibility gap

Unverified scans produce noise. Blind spots produce breaches.

Organizations accumulate forgotten subdomains, exposed test databases, and deprecated TLS configurations. Scanning without verified ownership creates authorization liabilities, while ad-hoc testing misses newly exposed ports and configuration drift.

Architecture

Structured five-stage reconnaissance pipeline

Stage 1

Passive Subdomain Discovery

Queries Certificate Transparency logs and resolves live A (IPv4) and AAAA (IPv6) records without touching target hosts directly.

discovery.py, resolver.py
Stage 2

Active Host Probing

Validates HTTP/HTTPS availability on ports 80/443 with SSRF defenses blocking private/loopback IP ranges and a 64 KB response streaming cap.

prober.py, scan_common.py
Stage 3

Lightweight TCP Port Scanning

Connects asynchronously across sixteen default service ports to classify open states and collect non-intrusive service greetings.

portscan.py
Stage 4

TLS & Header Inspection

Evaluates TLS certificate validity, deprecation, and defensive security headers like HSTS, CSP, and X-Content-Type-Options.

tls_inspect.py, headers_inspect.py
Stage 5

Heuristic Risk Scoring

Groups findings into severity tiers from Critical to Info, ordered by severity tier, score impact, then host.

scoring.py

Capabilities

Built for operational security rigor

Automated Change Detection

Differential engine compares consecutive scans to detect new subdomains, newly open ports, and certificate problems (expired, untrusted, self-signed, hostname mismatch).

Scheduled Cadences

Configurable automated scan intervals from 6 hours to 720 hours (30 days) with randomized execution jitter to disperse network load.

Transactional Email Alerts

Transactional outbox delivery issues notification digests to up to 5 configured recipients upon new risk discoveries.

Append-Only Audit Log

Append-only audit log: a database trigger blocks updates, deletes and truncates from the application.

Multi-Tenant Isolation

Role-based access control with owner, admin, and viewer ranks, enforced by tenant-scoped database queries and anti-enumeration defenses.

Authorization Gate

Ownership verification before active scanning

Active scans run only on domains you have verified with a DNS TXT record at _asm-verify.<domain>. A time-limited operator override exists for exceptional cases.

DNS TXT VERIFICATION RECORD {{ txt_label }} IN TXT "{{ txt_example }}"
